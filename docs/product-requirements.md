# Product Requirements

## Objective

Turn a research request into practical, evidence-supported information that any intended user can understand and implement.

## Intake

Capture the exact question, intended outcome, project goals, requirements, category tags, constraints, audience, requested order, and format. Reuse supplied information. Ask a focused clarification only when ambiguity materially changes the work. Keep interpretations separate from the original wording.

## Results and Accessibility

Show the exact question, direct answer, credible approaches, and practical instructions before optional technical depth. Preserve the user's order when explicitly supplied. Keep evidence and methodology available without requiring them to be read first.

## Approach Structure

Each approach explains purpose, suitability, prerequisites, ordered steps, expected outcome, verification, relevant tradeoffs, and evidence. Do not invent alternatives to reach a fixed count.

## Category Relevance

Include material that answers the question, supports an approach, establishes a prerequisite, explains a constraint, or verifies a result. Explain necessary cross-category dependencies. Omit unrelated search material.

## Evidence

Retain source identity, retrievable reference, date or version, supporting passage or result, applicability, limitations, verification status, and practical implication. Distinguish source findings from assumptions. Record material disagreement.

## Progress

Use understandable stages: defining the request; gathering information; checking findings; preparing instructions; reviewing delivery. Progress must reflect actual work.

## Constructive Neutrality

Goal-positive means helping accomplish the task, not hiding unfavorable evidence. Avoid unrelated judgments, predetermined conclusions, unsupported certainty, and silent changes to objectives or order.

## Exports

Preserve the question, order, prerequisites, instructions, references, material limitations, research date, and versions across formats.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The exact question is answered within scope.
- Findings remain aligned with goals and categories.
- Material claims have identifiable support.
- Meaningful supported alternatives are included.
- Instructions include prerequisites, ordered steps, expected outcomes, and checks.
- Methodology and material gaps are disclosed.
- The opening answer is accessible without technical background.

## Status

These are requirements and templates, not a claim that the application has been implemented or independently validated.
