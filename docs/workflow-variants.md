## Selection and execution: editorial guidance

1. Record Docs or AgentDocs, the source version, retrieval need, and applicable identifiers in the request record before substantive execution. Pending selection is not a third executable variant.
2. For AgentDocs tasks requiring retrieval, conduct appropriate web research before factual synthesis. Record source provenance, verification basis, failures, and limitations.
3. Leave tier unassigned unless the source taxonomy is supplied or a local scheme is explicitly identified. Do not imply that a local tier scheme came from MIA.
4. If required retrieval cannot occur, record the affected blocker and do not mark that step complete. Do not silently switch workflows.
5. Docs has no separately numbered retrieval step; this does not prohibit research required by the task.
6. Preserve source numbering and distinguish source text from CERBERUS interpretation. Record a conflict rather than silently selecting precedence.
7. The labels Mandatory Address, Princess Mia, Acknowledged, and Under Princess Mia do not supply their missing contents. Do not infer forms of address, identities, permissions, or instructions. Obtain actual text or record the affected requirement as unverifiable; block only work materially dependent on that missing text.
8. Delivering a response does not authorize publication, deployment, repository modification, messaging, or permission changes. Each consequential external action needs separate exact-target/content authorization.

## Relationship to CERBERUS methodology: proposed crosswalk

| CERBERUS research phase | Related variant steps |
| --- | --- |
| Scope definition | Docs W-1-W-5; AgentDocs W-1-W-2 and W-4-W-6 |
| Information gathering | AgentDocs W-3; task-required retrieval under Docs |
| Synthesis and verification | Docs W-3 and W-7; AgentDocs W-4 and W-8 |
| Practical structuring | Docs W-6; AgentDocs W-7 |
| QA and delivery | Docs W-7-W-8; AgentDocs W-8-W-9 |

This is a responsibility crosswalk, not a reordered execution schedule. AgentDocs W-3 remains before W-4-W-6 even though those steps also concern scope. Existing project-first research guidance and AgentDocs web-first retrieval must be reconciled explicitly for a task; do not claim identical sequences or full conformance while the conflict is unresolved. These variants do not rename the separate eight-stage governance contract or establish an enforced release gate.

## Review outcomes and limitations

Use Pass, Partial, Fail, or Not applicable per requirement, with an output excerpt or source locator. Justify non-applicability. These are local review outcomes, not stage-progress statuses or certification. A completed record does not prove factual accuracy or human approval.

Companion files, named-directive text, complete architecture, enforcement, and source-tier definitions remain subject to the limitations in [source material register](source-material-register.md).
