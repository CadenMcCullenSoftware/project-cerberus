# Quality-Assurance Checklist

## Fidelity
- [ ] Exact question and intended outcome are preserved.
- [ ] Goals, category relevance, constraints, and requested order are respected.

## Evidence
- [ ] Material claims have identifiable support.
- [ ] Relevant dates, versions, and applicability are checked.
- [ ] Findings are distinguished from assumptions.
- [ ] Material conflicts and gaps are disclosed.
- [ ] Claimed tool actions actually occurred.

## Actionability
- [ ] Supported alternatives are included where available.
- [ ] Prerequisites and dependencies are explicit.
- [ ] Steps are ordered and usable.
- [ ] Expected results and verification are provided.

## Accessibility
- [ ] Practical information precedes optional technical depth.
- [ ] Necessary terms are explained.
- [ ] Essential qualifications remain visible.
- [ ] Unrelated opinions are removed.

## Integrity
- [ ] Export preserves essential context.
- [ ] External changes have authorization.
- [ ] Unmet criteria and review status are recorded.

## Source Integration and Workflow
- [ ] Source authorship, attribution, versions, and authority boundaries are preserved.
- [ ] Supplied wording is distinguishable from summaries and CERBERUS adaptations.
- [ ] The selected workflow is recorded; pending selection is not reported as completed execution.
- [ ] Workflow identifiers are qualified by variant and original order is preserved.
- [ ] Required retrieval occurred, or the affected limitation/blocker is recorded.
- [ ] Source tiers use an identified taxonomy or remain unassigned.
- [ ] Missing governing material is listed without reconstruction or unsupported absence claims.
- [ ] Manifesto commitments are not presented as demonstrated implementation.
- [ ] Framework-to-subject analysis stays within the explicitly requested scope.
- [ ] Delivery is not represented as authorization for another external action.
- [ ] Policy adoption is separate from approval to commit documentation.
